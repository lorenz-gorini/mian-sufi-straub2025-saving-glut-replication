Communication job
By the end, the course audience should view the mortgage channel as the most promising research extension because same-vintage preliminary evidence shows mortgage borrowing dominated the pre-2008 debt and saving changes below the top, while the Figure 6 diagnostics show a persistent upper-middle saving decline that is not just percentile relabeling.

Narrative
1. State the extension thesis and evidence boundary.
2. Show borrower-side mortgage versus consumer-credit stock changes.
3. Connect those stocks to write-down-adjusted active-saving contributions.
4. Use the full Leontief result to separate ultimate lenders from borrowers.
5. Re-index exact-method Figure 6 saving rates by mean real wealth.
6. Show trailing-five-year Figure 6 saving rates across percentile and time.
7. Convert the descriptive pattern into a bounded causal research agenda.

- Post-1982 80th percentile: mean wealth $286k and mean annual net saving 7.4%.

- Top-1 trailing-five-year peak: 68.5% in the window ending 2008.

Interpretation boundary
The evidence is descriptive accounting, not a causal estimate. The wealth-level comparison uses cohort means and does not hold wealth fixed within bins. Consumer credit is the old-kit aggregate and does not separately identify cards, student loans, vehicles, or medical debt. The same-vintage matrix is coarsened to eight intermediary rows and retains an explicit unassigned owner.
