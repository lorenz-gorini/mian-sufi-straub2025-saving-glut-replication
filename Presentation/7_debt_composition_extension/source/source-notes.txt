Communication job
By the end, the course audience should view the mortgage channel as the most
promising research extension because same-vintage preliminary evidence shows
that mortgage borrowing dominated the pre-2008 debt and saving changes below
the top, while the unveiling assigns increasing net mortgage claims to the top
1 percent.

Narrative
1. State the extension thesis and evidence boundary.
2. Show borrower-side mortgage versus consumer-credit stock changes.
3. Connect those stocks to write-down-adjusted active-saving contributions.
4. Use the full Leontief result to separate ultimate lenders from borrowers.
5. Convert the descriptive pattern into a bounded causal research agenda.

Interpretation boundary
The evidence is descriptive accounting, not a causal estimate. Consumer credit
is the old-kit aggregate and does not separately identify credit cards,
student loans, vehicles, or medical debt. The same-vintage matrix is coarsened
to eight intermediary rows and retains an explicit unassigned owner.
