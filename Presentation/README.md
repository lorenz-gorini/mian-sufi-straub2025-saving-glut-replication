# Presentation Collection

The project uses separate presentation folders for distinct teaching or
validation purposes. This prevents concurrent work on one topic from
overwriting another topic's editable source. Each deck must be internally
consistent, readable, reproducible, and narrow enough that its purpose is clear
from its folder.

The deck is not the source of truth for task status, methods, or numeric results.
It summarizes explanations maintained in markdown and outputs generated by
code.

## Pedagogical contract

Every substantive method or result should answer, in this order:

1. What economic object are we trying to measure?
2. Why is it not directly observed?
3. What equation or accounting relationship identifies it?
4. What does every symbol mean, and in which units?
5. Which files and columns operationalize those symbols?
6. Which transformations, assumptions, and sign conventions are required?
7. Which invariant or comparison tells us the implementation is correct?
8. What does the resulting figure imply economically?

Use small numerical examples, balance sheets, matrix diagrams, or flow diagrams
when they make these answers easier to understand. Visuals must explain actual
logic rather than decorate the slide.

## Folder contract

- `1_replication_package_validation/` contains the old-package-versus-2025
  validation deck.
- `2_data-map/` contains only the data architecture: source roles, mappings,
  transformations, figure dependencies, and storage/evidence workflow.
- `3_theoretical_methodology/` contains the separate theoretical methodology
  deck, including the Leontief derivation, exact worked network, and the
  paper's key conceptual innovation.
- `4_figure_comparison/` contains the empirical comparison deck. It keeps the
  2025 target, authors-old Stata precursor, ours-public robustness build, and
  ours-authors reconstruction visibly separate.
- `math-assets/` contains the shared LaTeX source for equations embedded in
  the editable PowerPoint decks.
- The root `presentation.tex` and `presentation.pdf` are a legacy combined
  progress deck. They are retained for provenance but are no longer the
  canonical editable entry point for new topic-specific work.

Never have separate chats edit the same presentation source. New material goes
into the numbered folder whose topic owns it.

## Workflow

- Give each deck its own 16:9 entry point and compiled output inside its
  numbered subfolder.
- Keep the deck current as material outputs are produced; do not wait until the
  project is complete.
- Document a step in markdown before or together with adding its slide.
- Generate figures and tables programmatically and include them from stable
  subfolders. Never hand-type changing estimates into slides.
- Keep detailed diagnostics, schema tables, and robustness checks in an
  appendix.
- Record the producing script beside every generated artifact or in a small
  manifest.

## Recommended sequence across decks

1. Research question, paper-version alignment, and approved scope.
2. A common map from household saving through intermediaries to final uses.
3. **Figure 1:** direct ownership, intermediary cross-holdings, the unveiling
   equations, a small matrix example, and the measure of indirect ownership.
4. **Figure 5:** wealth changes versus valuation effects, distributional saving,
   aggregation into top 1/bottom 99, the 1982 normalization, and the 10-year
   moving average.
5. **Figure 8:** unveiled household-debt assets, household liabilities, the net
   debt definition \(ND_{it}=A^D_{it}-D_{it}\), and its sign interpretation.
6. Authors' benchmark versus our reconstruction for each core figure, including
   discrepancies.
7. **Figure 9, only if time permits:** convert Figure 8 debt stocks into
   10-year-moving-average financing flows.
8. Limitations, unresolved version differences, and lessons learned.

Each equation slide should contain:

- the equation;
- a compact symbol-to-data mapping;
- units and signs;
- a one-sentence verbal interpretation;
- the implementation or validation step it motivates.

Substantive equations in PowerPoint must be generated from
`math-assets/equations.tex` and embedded as aspect-ratio-preserving vector
graphics. Do not approximate sums, fractions, matrices, subscripts, or
superscripts with Unicode text. If the paper equation and a computationally
equivalent block form are both useful, label the paper equation first and
state the equivalence explicitly.

## Visual conventions

Adapt the useful pattern from the `Podcasts-Schwarz` project:

- white background with dark navy text;
- one restrained accent color used consistently;
- minimal academic layout and generous whitespace;
- one claim or methodological idea per slide;
- short, declarative slide titles that state the takeaway and remain on one
  line; if a title wraps, shorten it before reducing it below the deck's title
  size;
- a visible hierarchy of **title → primary visual/equation → implication →
  source**, with whitespace between those layers;
- one dominant visual or equation per slide; definitions and explanations are
  supporting material and must not compete with the takeaway;
- a restrained content-title size, a thin rule below the title, and a page
  number in the upper-right corner;
- a dedicated implication band for the slide's only main message, separated
  from a smaller source rail with safe bottom and side margins;
- no persistent footer text that resembles clipped body copy;
- one stable notation and color mapping across Figures 1, 5, 8, and 9;
- consistent axes, units, wealth-group colors, and ordering across figures;
- `booktabs` tables, no vertical rules, and slide-specific compact table
  variants when the full table is too dense;
- small source/sample note on each empirical slide;
- explicit visual distinction among **authors' benchmark**,
  **our reconstruction**, and **difference**.

Diagrams must use anchored labels and deliberate spacing. Matrix braces,
row/column labels, arrows, and operators must attach unambiguously to the object
they describe. Never leave floating plus/equality signs between independent
text boxes, and never allow connectors, labels, or nodes to overlap. Render and
inspect every slide at presentation size after any material edit.

The PowerPoint in `1_replication_package_validation/` defines the current
visual grammar: Calibri/Calibri Light, dark navy, one orange accent, bounded
content panels, a pale implication band, and a separate source rail. Apply this
grammar to new decks unless the user explicitly approves another theme.

Use vector figures (`.pdf` or `.svg` converted safely for LaTeX) when practical.
If a figure is rasterized, ensure labels remain readable at presentation size.
Quantitative network labels must remain at least as legible as the surrounding
body text; their size cannot depend on zooming into the slide.

## Current builds

### Theoretical methodology

The canonical full theory deliverable is the editable 16-slide PowerPoint
`3_theoretical_methodology/presentation.pptx`. A separate seven-slide companion,
`3_theoretical_methodology/presentation_short.pptx`, retains only the minimum
methodology sequence needed for combination with the data and empirical-result
modules. The earlier Beamer source and PDF remain in the same folder as
historical references; they are not synchronized with the current PowerPoint
hierarchy. The full deck explains:

- why direct balance sheets stop at the first holder;
- the issuer-row, holder-column orientation and row-sum invariant;
- Equation (4), the direct-ownership construction;
- the owner and intermediary blocks \(B_t\) and \(Q_t\);
- the path expansion, convergence condition, and Leontief-style solve;
- a complete four-sector network in which all six directed edges are visible;
- the exact reconciliation
  \(20\%+52.093\%=72.093\%\) for ultimate household ownership of the bank;
- Equations (2)--(3), the readable Python solve, and the relationship to the
  authors' older seven-round Stata procedure.

The current PowerPoints use exact, embedded network diagrams rather than
decorative artwork and LaTeX-rendered SVG equations from
`math-assets/equations.tex`. Equation (4) therefore retains the paper's
row-scaling operator rather than a Unicode or `diag` shorthand. The full
deck's block-matrix slide explicitly attaches owner and intermediary
dimensions to rows and columns; the worked example shows the direct 20%,
indirect 52.093%, and unveiled 72.093% household shares in one visual sequence.
All 16 full slides and all seven condensed slides were rendered and inspected
on 2026-08-10; both template-fidelity checks passed without issues.

To rebuild the historical Beamer artifact only, run from
`Presentation/3_theoretical_methodology/`:

```bash
latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error presentation.tex
```

See `3_theoretical_methodology/README.md` for the scope and asset-provenance
contract.

### Empirical figure comparison

The editable 12-slide PowerPoint
`4_figure_comparison/presentation.pptx` establishes a four-role naming and
evidence hierarchy and applies it to Figures 1, 5, and 8. Its central tests use
February 2021 authors-kit inputs with the July 2025 definitions wherever those
inputs identify the revised object. Figure 1 and Figure 5 are labeled
reconstructions; Figure 8 remains a bounded proxy because the old kit does not
contain the completed revised matrices. The regenerated old Figure 6 from
Stata is a related authors-old precursor, while the later public-data Figure 1
build is a robustness result. Paper graphs are shown without their printed
captions, and their descriptions are restated in editable slide text. All 12
slides were rendered and inspected on 2026-08-10. The deck passes template
fidelity, overflow, unresolved-placeholder, source-note, and archive-integrity
checks. See `4_figure_comparison/README.md` for its evidence contract.

## Separate replication-package validation deck

`1_replication_package_validation/replication_package_validation.pptx` is a
separate ten-slide PowerPoint whose sole purpose is to compare the regenerated
February 2021 authors-package outputs with the closest July 2025 paper
exhibits. It does not replace the other topic modules.

The main sequence now stops after the direct comparisons of old Figure 7 with
2025 Figure 8 and old Figure 9 with 2025 Figure 10. A visible appendix divider
moves the weaker comparisons out of the current presentation while preserving
them for future use. The two old benchmark figures are regenerated from the
same Stata series with inside legends and an 8:5 aspect ratio; this changes
layout only and prevents the legend from shrinking the data panel. The revised
deck was rendered from the exported PowerPoint and all ten slides were
visually inspected on 2026-08-10.
