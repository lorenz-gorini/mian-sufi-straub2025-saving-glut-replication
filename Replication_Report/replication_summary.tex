\compactheading{Method and data}

The empirical problem is to move from observed holders to \emph{ultimate}
owners. For direct ownership matrix $\bar M_t$---rows are issuers and columns
are holders---the revised paper sums every intermediary path. With $Q_t$ the
intermediary block and $B_t$ the direct exits to terminal owners, our Python
implementation solves
\[
  \Omega_t=(I-Q_t)^{-1}B_t, \qquad \rho(Q_t)<1.
\]
Financial Accounts/FWTW matrices identify bilateral stocks; DINA allocates the
household sector across wealth groups; NIPA closes aggregate income and saving;
and asset returns separate active saving from valuation changes,
$\Theta_{g,t}=\sum_a(W^a_{g,t}-W^a_{g,t-1}-\pi^a_tW^a_{g,t-1})$.

We first ran the complete February 2021 Stata master under StataNow SE 19.5.
After portability-only path changes, dependency bootstrapping, and suppressing
one interactive browser call, it completed and regenerated the major outputs
for the paper version it accompanies. That package generally ends in 2016 and
uses seven stage-specific unveiling rounds. The 2025 paper instead reports 34
instruments, 23 intermediaries plus four ultimate owners, a revised completion,
a full matrix/Leontief solve, and data through 2019. We therefore reimplemented
the 2025 definitions in Python from the earliest usable supplied inputs and
label every remaining version boundary.

\compactheading{Interpretation and reproducibility boundary}

These are version-bounded reconstructions, not exact numerical reproductions.
Figures 1 and 5 recover the main 2025 patterns from older author-supplied
inputs. For Figure 8, restarting from saved direct cells and applying the full
inverse yields 2007 top-1 lending of
$\SummaryFigureEightPrimaryTopTwoThousandSeven$ pp, versus
$\SummaryFigureEightPaperTopTwoThousandSeven$ pp in the paper, while bottom-99
debt is $\SummaryFigureEightPrimaryBottomTwoThousandSeven$ pp versus
$\SummaryFigureEightPaperBottomTwoThousandSeven$ pp. Holding the 2021 vintage
as fixed as the saved fields permit, replacing seven rounds with the full
inverse changes the two series by only
$\SummaryFigureEightOperatorTopMeanAbsDiff$ and
$\SummaryFigureEightOperatorBottomMeanAbsDiff$ pp on average. The remaining
gap is therefore mainly a data-vintage, completion, taxonomy, and allocation
boundary rather than finite-round truncation. A current-public-FWTW rerun is a
separate robustness exercise, not an exact recovery of the authors' values.

All empirical series, comparisons, and displayed metrics are generated by
code. The principal entry points are
\path{build_figure1_authors_data.py},
\path{build_figure5_authors_data.py}, and
\path{build_figure8_2021_direct.py}; tests cover matrix conservation,
group partitions, valuation/saving closure, normalization, and debt signs.
